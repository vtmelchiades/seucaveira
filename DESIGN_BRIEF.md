## Build constraints
- Honor constraint: Absolute dark room theme (#040404) with zero cream/off-white backgrounds
- Honor constraint: No italic accent words in headlines, no numbered 01/02/03 section labels, no monospace labels, no pill-shaped buttons
- Honor constraint: No SaaS neon purple/blue gradients, no generic backdrop-blur 10% white border glassmorphism cards, no Lucide icons in colored circles, no symmetrical 3-equal-column layouts
- Honor constraint: 100% procedural GLSL raymarching SDF shader + interactive NLE Mastering Bar with 3 real-time post-processing states
